# 看见她 Unseen

一款关于多囊卵巢综合征（PCOS）的诊断科普游戏。
A bilingual (中文 / English) educational game about polycystic ovary syndrome.

**在线游玩 / Play:** https://zahra666.github.io/unseen_game/

## 中文

PCOS 很常见，但很多有 PCOS 的女性一直没有被诊断。症状常被当成"痘痘""胖了""压力大"，或者"女孩子月经不准很正常"。

在这个游戏里，你是社区诊所的医生。每天随机来 3 位女性，你要在有限的门诊时间里安排问诊、查体和检查，判断她是否患有 PCOS，或者另有病因。

- **三档难度：** 简单（典型表现，线索一致）、中等（病因被掩盖，需要鉴别）、困难（多病共存，记录缺失）
- **鹿特丹标准：** 三项满足两项，并排除妊娠、甲状腺疾病、高泌乳素血症等其他病因
- **病例解析：** 每天结束统一结算，判错的病例会展开解析和随访结局
- **隐藏彩蛋：** 困难模式里藏着一家三代的故事

## English

PCOS is common, yet many women who have it are never diagnosed. Their symptoms get put down to acne, weight, stress, or "irregular periods are normal for girls".

You are the doctor at a community clinic. Three women are drawn at random each day. With limited clinic time, choose which questions, examinations and tests to order, then decide whether she has PCOS or another condition.

- **Three difficulty levels:** typical presentations; hidden causes and differentials; overlapping disease with missing records
- **Rotterdam criteria:** two of three features, after excluding pregnancy, thyroid disease, hyperprolactinaemia and other causes
- **Case reviews:** each clinic day ends with a review; wrong calls open a case explanation and follow-up outcome
- **Hidden story:** a three-generation family waits in hard mode

## 说明 / Disclaimer

所有病例与人物均为虚构，仅供医学科普，不构成诊疗建议。
All patients are fictional. For health education only; not medical advice.
