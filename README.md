# 看见她 Unseen

一款关于多囊卵巢综合征（PCOS）的科普生存游戏。
A bilingual (中文 / English) survival game that teaches the signs of polycystic ovary syndrome.

**在线游玩 / Play:** https://zahra666.github.io/unseen_game/

## 中文

PCOS（多囊卵巢综合征）很常见，大约每 10 个女性就有 1 个，但很多人一直不知道。月经很少、长粗毛、痘痘反复、头顶掉发，常常被当成"太累了""压力大""女孩子月经不准很正常"。

在这个游戏里，你是社区诊所新来的医生。每天随机来 3 位女性，你问问题、做检查，然后给她一个建议：可能是 PCOS 去查一下、可能是别的问题去看其他科，或者暂时没事。

- **生存玩法：** 建议对了拿工资；漏掉病人会被投诉。每天晚上要交房租，房租会涨。交不起房租就被赶出家门，被投诉 3 次就被辞退。看你能撑几天。
- **三档难度：** 简单（信号明显）、中等（有别的病来捣乱）、困难（好几个问题叠在一起）
- **记住信号：** 游戏结束时会给你一张"记住这些信号"的卡片，可以一键转给在意的人
- **隐藏彩蛋：** 困难模式里藏着一家三代的故事

游戏想传达的只有一句话：**这些信号出现 2–3 个，就去看医生。**

## English

PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome) is common: about 1 in 10 women have it, and many never find out. Rare periods, coarse hair, stubborn spots and thinning hair get brushed off as tiredness, stress, or "normal for girls".

You are the new doctor at a community clinic. Three women come in each day. Ask questions, order tests, then give your advice: could be PCOS, get checked; something else, see another specialist; or nothing to worry about for now.

- **Survival:** right advice earns pay; missed patients bring complaints. Rent is due every night and keeps rising. Can't pay and you're evicted; three complaints and you're fired. How long can you last?
- **Three levels:** clear signs; other problems getting in the way; several problems at once
- **Remember the signs:** game over shows a card of PCOS signs you can send to someone you care about
- **Hidden story:** a three-generation family waits in hard mode

The game has one message: **if you notice two or three of these signs, see a doctor.**

## 隐私 / Privacy

游戏使用 [Umami](https://umami.is) 匿名统计游玩数据：访问人数、停留时长、所选难度、给出的建议和撑了几天。不使用 cookie，不收集任何个人信息，数据存储在欧盟服务器。
The game uses [Umami](https://umami.is) for anonymous play statistics: visits, time on page, difficulty, advice given and days survived. No cookies and no personal information; data is stored on EU servers.

The game is a single `index.html`; open it in any browser.
