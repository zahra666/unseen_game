# 看见她 Unseen

一款关于 PMOS（多内分泌代谢卵巢综合征，旧称多囊卵巢综合征 PCOS）的科普生存游戏。
A bilingual (中文 / English) survival game that teaches the signs of PMOS, the condition formerly called polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS).

**在线游玩 / Play:** https://zahra666.github.io/unseen_game/

## 中文

PMOS 以前叫多囊卵巢综合征（PCOS），2026 年改名，因为它不只是卵巢的问题，还和激素、代谢有关。它很常见，大约每 10 个女性就有 1 个，但很多人一直不知道。月经很少、长粗毛、痘痘反复、头顶掉发，常常被当成"太累了""压力大""女孩子月经不准很正常"。

在这个游戏里，你是社区诊所新来的医生。每天来 3 位女性，你问问题、做检查，然后给她一个建议：可能是 PMOS 去查一下、可能是别的问题去看其他科，或者暂时没事。

- **两套压力：**
  - **存款和房租：** 建议对了拿工资；每天晚上交房租（简单 RM 100、中等 RM 300、困难 RM 500）。交不起房租就被赶出家门。
  - **病人的信任度：** 问问题免费；检查会降低信任度，开太多她会觉得你在乱开单。信任度掉光，她生气走掉并投诉你。没乱开检查又建议对了，额外奖励 RM 50。
  - 漏诊或误诊也会被投诉，投诉满了就被辞退（简单 5 次、中等 4 次、困难 3 次）。看你能撑几天。
- **三档难度：** 简单（信号明显，小本子自动数信号，★ 标出推荐先问的问题）、中等（有别的病来捣乱，仍会自动数信号）、困难（好几个问题叠在一起，没有小帮手）
- **新手动画：** 第一次打开会自动播放 8 页动画，介绍 PMOS、玩法和信号手册，可以跳过，也能从菜单重新打开
- **18 个成就：** 比如"看见她""藏不住""望闻问切"，还有一个隐藏成就
- **记住信号：** 游戏结束时会给你一张"记住这些信号"的卡片，可以一键转给在意的人
- **隐藏彩蛋：** 困难模式里藏着一家三代的故事

游戏想传达的只有一句话：**这些信号出现 2–3 个，就去看医生。**

## English

PMOS was called polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) until 2026, when it was renamed because it involves hormones and metabolism, not just the ovaries. It is common: about 1 in 10 women have it, and many never find out. Rare periods, coarse hair, stubborn spots and thinning hair get brushed off as tiredness, stress, or "normal for girls".

You are the new doctor at a community clinic. Three women come in each day. Ask questions, order tests, then give your advice: could be PMOS, get checked; something else, see another specialist; or nothing to worry about for now.

- **Two kinds of pressure:**
  - **Savings and rent:** right advice earns pay; rent is due every night (RM 100, 300 or 500 by difficulty). Can't pay and you're evicted.
  - **Patient trust:** questions are free; every test lowers her trust. If it runs out she walks out and complains. Get it right without overtesting for a RM 50 bonus.
  - Missed or wrong diagnoses bring complaints too; hit the limit (5, 4 or 3) and you're fired. How long can you last?
- **Three levels:** clear signs with an automatic sign counter and ★ tips; other problems in the way, still with the counter; several problems at once, no helpers
- **Animated intro:** plays on first visit to explain PMOS, the rules and the sign guide; skippable and replayable from the menu
- **18 achievements**, including one hidden
- **Remember the signs:** game over shows a card of PMOS signs you can send to someone you care about
- **Hidden story:** a three-generation family waits in hard mode

The game has one message: **if you notice two or three of these signs, see a doctor.**

## 关于改名 / About the new name

2026 年 5 月，国际医学界在《柳叶刀》上宣布把 PCOS 改名为 PMOS。2026–2028 年是过渡期，国际疾病分类和指南会在 2028 年前完成更新，诊断标准没有变。游戏里同时写出新旧名字，方便玩家在医院里沟通。
In May 2026 an international consortium announced in *The Lancet* that PCOS is renamed PMOS. 2026–2028 is a transition period, with disease classifications and guidelines due to update by 2028; diagnostic criteria are unchanged. The game shows both names so players can talk to their doctors.

## 隐私 / Privacy

游戏使用 [Umami](https://umami.is) 匿名统计游玩数据：访问人数、停留时长、所选难度、给出的建议、检查用了多少信任度和撑了几天。不使用 cookie，不收集任何个人信息，数据存储在欧盟服务器。
The game uses [Umami](https://umami.is) for anonymous play statistics: visits, time on page, difficulty, advice given, trust spent on tests and days survived. No cookies and no personal information; data is stored on EU servers.
